# CROR Signal Aspects — Rules 405–439
Source: Canadian Rail Operating Rules (CROR), January 28, 2025 Version
Extracted from PDF pages 71–80 (Rules 404–439)

## Rule 404. Standard Indications
The illustrations in Rules 405–440 are standard aspects and indications. Other signal aspects and
indications necessary will be illustrated in special instructions.

## Rule 440. Direction Indicator
Flashing arrow indicators used in conjunction with block signals. When illuminated, identifies that
the route at the next controlled location is displaying a permissive signal and the route is lined
and secured as indicated by the direction of the arrow.
