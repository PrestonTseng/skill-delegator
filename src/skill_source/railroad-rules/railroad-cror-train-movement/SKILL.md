# CROR — Train Movement and Authority (OCS, TOP, Flagging)

## When to Use
CROR train movement authorization, OCS clearance, Track Occupancy Permits, meeting/passing rules,
flagging, or protection of track work. Full rule text in `references/cror-train-movement.md`.
For signal indications see `railroad-cror-signals`. For general rules see `railroad-cror-general`.

---

## OCS Key Rules (Rules 301–322)

| Rule | Key Requirement |
|------|----------------|
| 302 | Clearance required to enter/foul OCS territory. Both conductor and locomotive engineer must verify possession, designation, and **visually verify engine number** before acting |
| 302.1 | Clearance stays in effect until fulfilled, superseded, or cancelled. Fulfil in order issued |
| 302.2 | Superseding clearance that restricts existing authority: RTC must wait for **acknowledgement from both crew members** before proceeding |
| 302.3 | Cancel requires train to be: (a) clear of limits, (b) protected by Form T GBO, or (c) within cautionary limits. Not effective until acknowledged (repeat number + "cancelled" + RTC initials) |
| 303 | Max **two** trains same direction same limits, each protecting against the other; written understanding required before either moves |
| 303.1 | Following train: must not leave until preceding train reports leaving an identifiable point ahead; report recorded in writing. Without report: REDUCED speed (≤25 MPH) |
| 304 | Restricted train must not leave until opposing trains named on clearance have physically arrived (engine + marker visible, or crew confirmation) |
| 304.1 | Must stop **clear of the fouling point** at meeting/clearing points |
| 305 | RTC must protect against all conflicting trains, transfers, and TOPs before issuing clearance |
| 308 | Work clearance: movement in either direction within named limits; stays in effect until superseded or cancelled |
| 308.1 | Proceed clearance: move only in specified direction. Exception: may reverse ≤300 feet if track seen clear |

## TOP Key Rules (Rules 849–856)

| Rule | Key Requirement |
|------|----------------|
| 849 | RTC must protect against all conflicting movements before issuing TOP |
| 851 | TOP within cautionary limits: effective from RTC acknowledgement by foreman until cancelled |
| 856 | Train entering TOP limits: crew must receive instructions **directly from the foreman**; repeat and acknowledge before acting |

## Flagging (Rules 35–41)

| Rule | Key Requirement |
|------|----------------|
| 35 | Emergency hazard: flag in each direction ≥**2 miles**. Red flag by day, lighted red fusee by night. Continue until: (i) whistle acknowledgement, or (ii) stop, or (iii) flagman's location reached. Movement stopped by flagman must not proceed until instructed by flagman |
| 36 | Subdivisions ≤30 MPH max: minimum flagging distance reduced to **1 mile** |
| 11 (fusee) | Movement approaching red fusee: proceed at REDUCED speed to ≥**2 miles beyond** the fusee |

## Track Work Protection (Rules 42–44)

- **Rule 42/842:** Foreman must have written authority before allowing employees within protected limits. Main track protection via: (a) stop movements in advance, or (b) GBO providing protection.
- **Rule 43/843:** Slow track: maintain restriction until movement has completely passed protected zone.
- **Rule 44:** Unusual track/signal conditions or limited visibility → **RESTRICTED speed** unless more favorable signal permits.

## Special Movement Rules

| Rule | Key Point |
|------|-----------|
| 82 | Must not exceed limits of authority; stop short of conflicting movement or Stop signal |
| 104.1 | Spring switch: crew member looks back immediately to confirm switch closed after movement passes |
| 104.2 | Dual control in "hand" mode: crew member positions to throw/restore; restore to normal after each use |
| 104.3 | Power switch at Stop signal: must not operate until RTC authorizes movement |
| 105 | Main track (OCS): must not enter without clearance, cautionary limits confirmation, or RTC confirmation of no conflict |
| 107 | Passenger station: locomotive engineer must not start until proper proceed signal from conductor, or scheduled departure time (whichever later) |
